Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Documint Hub (developed by Empire Pixels Pro) handles information when you use our Android application. Documint Hub has no backend server — all of your documents and settings stay safely on your device. By using the app, you agree to the use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Target Audience & Age Appropriateness

Documint Hub is intended for a general audience of all ages — freelancers, small businesses and students creating invoices, quotations and receipts. We do not collect or harvest personal identifying information from any users, regardless of age.

2. Geographical Distribution Exclusion

This application is distributed globally through the Google Play Store, with the explicit exception of Israel (IL). The service is not licensed or supported for use within this region.

3. Information the App Accesses

The app accesses the following only on your device, and only with your permission where required:

  • Files & Photos (optional): Used only when you pick a company logo or a QR payment image, or when you export a backup file. These are stored on your own device and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Camera / Storage (optional): Only to select an image file for your logo or QR payment code.
  • Internet: Used solely to load advertisements (Google AdMob) and to check for app updates. Document creation, editing and export work fully offline.
  • What we do NOT collect: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, contacts, location, calendar contents, files or unique device identifiers to any server we operate — the app has no backend of its own.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, edit and export your invoices, quotations and receipts as PDF files.
  • To store your business profile, client list and document history locally on your device.
  • To keep your free monthly document quota and settings (language, theme, numbering).
  • To process your subscription securely via Google Play Billing (Monthly and Yearly plans) — payment details are never seen or stored by us.

5. Advertising

Documint Hub may display Google AdMob banner advertisements. Google may collect advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy policies. Advertisements never appear inside your generated documents or exported PDFs, and they are removed automatically for PRO subscribers.

You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. No other data is shared with advertisers.

6. Data Security & Deletion

All app data (documents, clients, business profile, bank details and settings) is stored locally on your device. You can move it to another device — or restore it after a reinstall — at any time using Settings → Data, Backup & Privacy → Save to File / Open from File.

Data Deletion: Users retain full control over their data. You can erase everything instantly from Settings → Delete Account & Wipe Data, by clearing app storage, or by uninstalling the app. Because the app has no server, there is no account record held by us to delete.
